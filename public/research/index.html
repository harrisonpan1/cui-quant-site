Research

Option surfaces and implied information

  • Tighter bounds for implied volatility based on the Dirac Delta family method — Journal of Futures Markets (2025).
  • A new representation of the risk-neutral distribution and its applications — Quantitative Finance (2022).
  • A model-free Fourier cosine method for estimating the risk-neutral density — Journal of Derivatives (2021).

Pricing and computational methods

  • A general valuation framework for SABR and stochastic local volatility models — SIAM Journal on Financial Mathematics (2018).
  • Pricing discretely monitored barrier options under Markov processes using Markov chain approximations — Journal of Derivatives (2021).
  • Analysis of Markov chain approximation for Asian options and occupation-time derivatives: Greeks and convergence rates — Mathematical Methods of Operations Research (2021).
  • Efficient simulation of generalized SABR and stochastic local volatility models based on Markov chain approximations — European Journal of Operational Research (2021).
  • Windowed complex Fourier series for multi-asset contingent claims — under review (2026).

Equity and volatility derivatives

  • Implied Willow Tree — Journal of Derivatives (2024).
  • VIX Options Valuation via Continuous-time Markov Chain Approximation and Ito-Taylor Expansion — Journal of Derivatives (2024).
  • Valuation and calibration of VIX options and target volatility options with affine GARCH models — Journal of Futures Markets (2020).

Insurance and annuities

  • Valuation of Guaranteed Lifelong Withdrawal Benefit with Long-term Care Option — Insurance: Mathematics and Economics (2024).
  • Efficient Pricing and Greeks Calculation for Multi-Asset Variable Annuities under a Multivariate OUSV Model — Insurance: Mathematics and Economics (2026).
