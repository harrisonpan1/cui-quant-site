## Zhenyu Cui

Zhenyu Cui is an Associate Professor (with tenure) in Financial Engineering at Stevens Institute of Technology and Director of the Financial Engineering Ph.D. program. His research spans derivatives pricing, volatility modeling, computational finance, stochastic simulation, portfolio methods and actuarial applications.

He received a Ph.D. in Statistics and an M.S. in Quantitative Finance from the University of Waterloo, and a B.S. in Actuarial Science from the University of Hong Kong.

## Website objective

This site is designed to make research easier to evaluate by quantitative teams. It should communicate:

- what problem a method solves;
- what data it requires;
- what it returns;
- what assumptions and numerical limits apply;
- what evidence has been reproduced;
- how an external team can test it.
